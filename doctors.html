Chief Dental Surgeon

Dr. Sajna

BDS · Chief Dental Surgeon
Aesthetic & Restorative Dentistry | Endodontics | Biomimetic Principles

Dr. Sajna leads Confidental Multispeciality Clinic with a deep commitment to patient comfort, clinical precision, and conservative oral healthcare.

Her clinical expertise spans aesthetic smile designing, advanced bonded restorations, crowns and bridges, root canal treatments, and comprehensive restorative dentistry.

Dr. Sajna's practice philosophy is centred on the preservation of natural tooth structure. She takes the time to thoroughly assess each patient's condition, listen to their concerns, and explain every treatment option with clarity and care.

With a gentle clinical touch and a passion for modern, evidence-based dentistry, Dr. Sajna ensures that every patient feels relaxed, supported, and confident in their smile.

“Dentistry is not just about procedures — it is about restoring comfort, preserving natural beauty, and ensuring every patient leaves with a confident smile.”
